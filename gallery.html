Attraction Places

Maletsunyane Falls

The highest water fall in lesotho with the height of 192m, located in Semonkong Maseru District. It is special because it has mist and rainbow always. There are tourist activities like hiking, camping, pony riding and village tours.

Thaba-Bosiu

Thaba-Bosiu "mountain at Night" in Sesotho. It is a flat topped mountain (1804m high) located 24km east of Maseru. It was the fortress and capital of King Moshoeshoe 1, the founder of Basotho nation in 1824. The mountain is easy to defend - it has 8 springs and steep cliffs. Today it is Lesotho's most important historic monument and burial place of Basotho kings. Visitors can see ruins of the old villages, royal graves, and a small museum

Katse Dam

Katse dam is in Leribe District, in the malibamatso river. It is 185m high and was built in 1996. It is part of the Lesotho Highlands water to South Africa. It is also the second largest dam in Africa by volume and creates a huge lake (35km long). There are boats to ride, fishing, and also dam tours inside the dam

Kome Cave Dwellings

It is located in Berea District, 30km east of Teyateyaneng. They were built in the early 19th century (around 1820) by the Bataung and Basia tribes who hid in the caves to escape wars and cannibalism during lifaqane War. People still live there even today. It is a National heritage site. Visitors can see original huts, rock painting, and meet descendants of the first families.

Thabana Ntlenyana

It is the highest peak in Lesotho and also in the Southen Africa. It is located in the Maloti Mountains, near Sani Pass on the border with South Africa. The area has snow in winter and it is very cold. Tourists can take pictures, go hiking, climbing, and also camping.

Sehlabathebe National Park

Lesotho's first National Park made in 1969 located in Qacha's Nek. UNESCO site with rock towers, san rock paintings, and rare wildflowers. Good for hiking and camping.
